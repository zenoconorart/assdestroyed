Why is oxytocin studied in couples?

Controlled studies have examined couple conflict, romantic touch, jealousy, social support, sexual and partner interaction, and intimate-relationship appraisal. The findings are interesting but not uniformly positive, so the research library links directly to the papers rather than reducing them to product claims.

FAQ

Questions with sources.

How safe is oxytocin in published human research?

A systematic review of 38 randomised controlled trials reported generally good short-term tolerability of intranasal oxytocin under controlled research conditions. That evidence concerns study preparations and participants, not this research reagent, so it should not be read as a self-administration safety claim.

MacDonald et al., systematic review ↗
Has oxytocin actually been researched in romantic couples?

Yes. Human studies have examined couple conflict, romantic touch, jealousy, social support and partner interaction. Results depend on context, sex, task and study design.
